## The UK's Solar Sweet Spot

South East England receives the most solar irradiance in the UK — roughly **10-15% more** than the national average. An 800W system here generates **900-960 kWh/year**.

| Area | Annual kWh | Annual saving | Payback (£600) |
|------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Kent coast | 960 | £130 | 4.6 years |
| Sussex | 945 | £127 | 4.7 years |
| Surrey | 920 | £124 | 4.8 years |
| Hampshire | 930 | £125 | 4.8 years |
| Berkshire | 910 | £123 | 4.9 years |

## Why the South East Outperforms

1. **Lower latitude** (50-51°N) = higher sun angle
2. **Coastal effect** = less cloud cover than inland
3. **Drier climate** = more clear-sky days
4. **Longer summers** in terms of effective solar hours

### Is London part of the South East for solar purposes?

Broadly yes — [London](/blog/plug-in-solar-london-guide) generates similarly to Surrey/Kent. Urban shading from buildings is the main differentiator.

### What's the best time to install in the South East?

Spring (March-April) captures the most generation in your first year. But [September 2026](/blog/ecoflow-stream-uk-launch-september-2026) — when kits launch — is still excellent.
