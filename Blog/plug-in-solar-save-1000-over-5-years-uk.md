## The 5-Year Model

Let's model the 5-year savings from an 800W plug-in solar system purchased for £600, installed in a typical English location.

### Assumptions

- **Location**: Midlands (representative UK average)
- **System**: 800W, [garden mount at 35° south-facing](/blog/ground-mount-plug-in-solar)
- **Annual generation**: 850 kWh (PVGIS estimate)
- **Self-consumption**: 60% (mixed presence household)
- **Tariff**: Starting at 24.5p/kWh (Ofgem Q3 2026 cap), rising 3% annually
- **Panel degradation**: 0.5% per year
- **Export tariff**: 4p/kWh (SEG)

### Year-by-Year Breakdown

| Year | Generation | Self-consumed | Import saved | Export income | Total saved |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | 850 kWh | 510 kWh | £125 | £14 | £139 |
| 2 | 846 kWh | 507 kWh | £128 | £14 | £142 |
| 3 | 842 kWh | 505 kWh | £132 | £13 | £145 |
| 4 | 838 kWh | 503 kWh | £136 | £13 | £149 |
| 5 | 834 kWh | 500 kWh | £140 | £13 | £153 |
| **Total** | **4,210** | **2,525** | **£661** | **£67** | **£728** |

## So… £728, Not £1,000?

With average assumptions, a Midlands household saves **£728 over 5 years** — not quite £1,000. But the model is conservative. Here's how to reach £1,000+:

### Scenario A: Higher Self-Consumption (75%)
If you [work from home](/blog/plug-in-solar-home-office-uk) or use [smart automation](/blog/plug-in-solar-smart-home-automation-uk) to shift loads, 75% self-consumption pushes 5-year savings to **~£920**.

### Scenario B: Southern Location
Moving the model to the [South East](/blog/plug-in-solar-south-east-england-guide) (950 kWh/year) with 60% self-consumption gives **~£820**.

### Scenario C: Higher Self-Consumption + Southern
Combining both: South East, 75% self-consumption, 5-year total = **~£1,050+**. That's your £1,000.

### Scenario D: Time-of-Use Tariff
On [Octopus Agile](/blog/plug-in-solar-octopus-agile-uk), peak rates can hit 35p+. Displacing peak-rate imports with solar pushes savings to **~£1,100+** even in the Midlands.

## The 10-Year Picture

Over 10 years, every scenario comfortably exceeds £1,000:

| Scenario | 10-year savings | Net profit (after £600 kit) |
|----------|----------------|---------------------------|
| Conservative | £1,530 | £930 |
| High self-consumption | £1,940 | £1,340 |
| Southern + high SC | £2,200 | £1,600 |
| Agile tariff | £2,400+ | £1,800+ |

### What if energy prices drop?

Even if prices fall 20%, plug-in solar still pays back within 6-7 years. The UK's energy cost trend is upward, making solar increasingly valuable over time.

### What about inflation?

Energy prices tend to rise faster than general inflation, which actually improves solar economics over time.
