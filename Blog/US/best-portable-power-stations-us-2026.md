The US portable power station market has matured into four serious players plus a lot of noise. This guide covers the ones actually worth buying in 2026, what each is best at, and where marketing spec sheets diverge from real-world performance.

## The shortlist

### EcoFlow DELTA 2 — best all-rounder ($599)
1,024Wh LiFePO4. 1,800W AC output, 2,700W surge. 500W solar input via MC4. Best-in-class app monitoring, sensible ecosystem, expandable via DELTA 2 Extra Battery to 3,072Wh.

### Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 — best surge power ($799)
1,056Wh LiFePO4. 1,500W continuous, 2,400W SurgePad handles brief 2,400W+ loads (kettles, microwaves briefly). 600W solar input.

### Jackery Explorer 1000 Plus — best portability ($749)
1,264Wh LiFePO4. 2,000W output. Lighter than competitors (14kg). Weakest app of the four.

### Bluetti AC180 — best value ($549)
1,152Wh LiFePO4. 1,800W. Solid all-rounder, less brand-name premium than Anker/EcoFlow.

## What separates them in the real world

- **Standby drain**: EcoFlow DELTA 2 loses ~2% per week idle; Jackery loses 4–5%
- **App reliability**: EcoFlow > Anker > Bluetti > Jackery
- **Solar MPPT**: All four accept MC4; EcoFlow's MPPT is measurably better at partial shade
- **Repairability**: Bluetti offers user-replaceable batteries; others don't
