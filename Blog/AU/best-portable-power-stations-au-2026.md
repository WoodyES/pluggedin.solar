Australia's portable power station market is dominated by the same four global brands as the US and UK — EcoFlow, Anker, Jackery, Bluetti — plus a few AU-specific brands like Companion, Adventure Kings and Enerdrive. This guide covers the ones actually worth buying in 2026 for Grey Nomad, off-grid weekender, and home resilience use.

## The shortlist

### EcoFlow DELTA 2 — best all-rounder (A$999)
1,024Wh LiFePO4. Excellent app, expandable. Widely available on Amazon AU.

### Anker SOLIX C1000 — best surge power (A$1,299)
1,056Wh LiFePO4. 2,400W SurgePad handles the electric kettle brief peaks.

### Jackery Explorer 1000 Plus — best portability (A$1,199)
1,264Wh, 14kg — lightest of the four for its capacity.

### Bluetti AC180 — best value (A$899)
1,152Wh LiFePO4. Solid all-rounder without brand premium.

### Enerdrive ePOWER B-TEC — best for 4WD/caravan integration (A$1,500+)
Australian brand, designed for permanent 12V-system integration in caravans. Not portable, but the go-to for serious touring rigs.

## AU-specific buying notes

- **Outback heat testing**: EcoFlow and Bluetti derate cleanly at 40°C+ ambient; Jackery reduces max continuous output more aggressively.
- **AS/NZS 3112 plug type**: verify. Some imports ship with Schuko or US plugs — buy AU-configured SKUs.
- **Warranty in AU**: EcoFlow has AU warehouse-honoured 5-year warranty. Jackery similar. Bluetti's AU warranty presence is patchier.
