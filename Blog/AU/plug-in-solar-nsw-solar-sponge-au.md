NSW retailers introduced **Solar Sponge tariffs** in 2023 — a super-cheap daytime rate (typically 6–10c/kWh from 10am–3pm) designed to soak up midday solar surplus on the network. For a household with a portable battery, this is a massive arbitrage opportunity: buy cheap at 10am, sell back to yourself at 34c during peak evening.

## The tariff basics

Typical NSW time-of-use tariff (Ausgrid network) in 2026:

| Period | Rate |
|---|---|
| Peak (5-8 PM) | 55–65c/kWh |
| Shoulder (7-5 AM, 8-10 PM) | 25c/kWh |
| Off-peak (10 PM-7 AM) | 20c/kWh |
| **Solar Sponge (10 AM-3 PM)** | **6-10c/kWh** |

That 6-10c mid-day rate is often cheaper than typical FiT export rates. Households with a battery can charge from grid during the sponge window, then discharge into evening peak.

## The plug-in + battery play

- **1kWh portable battery** (EcoFlow DELTA 2, Anker SOLIX): $600–$800
- **400W portable panel**: $250–$400
- **Solar Sponge daily charge**: 1 kWh × ~8c = 8c cost
- **Peak displacement**: 1 kWh × 60c = 60c value
- **Net daily arbitrage**: ~50c
- **Annual saving**: ~$180 from arbitrage + ~$100 from solar self-consumption = **$280/year**

Payback: ~3 years on the combined kit. This is the strongest economic case for portable solar-battery combos in AU.

## Retailers currently offering Solar Sponge in NSW

Amber, OVO Energy AU, Origin (with add-on request), AGL EVo (for EV owners), Powershop.
