Boxing Day remains the biggest single shopping day on the Australian retail calendar, and plug-in and portable solar gear has become a regular fixture of the sales. Unlike Black Friday or Cyber Monday — both borrowed from the US retail calendar and increasingly common here too — Boxing Day sits right at the start of peak summer sun, which makes it genuinely useful timing rather than just another discount event. If you've been waiting to buy a portable power station or a set of panels for the caravan, this is one of the better windows in the calendar to do it.

## Why Boxing Day timing actually matters here

Plug-in solar kits sit outside the AS/NZS 4777 grid-connection framework that governs permanent rooftop systems — they're portable or off-grid setups by design, which is exactly why Boxing Day timing lines up so well:

- **You get the gear before peak summer generation**, rather than buying in autumn and missing months of good sun
- **Caravan and camping season is starting**, so Grey Nomad buyers are shopping for the same reason retailers are discounting
- **Retailers clear pre-Christmas stock** that didn't move during the lead-up, same mechanism as in other markets

If you already compared [Black Friday](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-black-friday-deals-2026-au) and [Cyber Monday / Click Frenzy](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-cyber-monday-click-frenzy-deals-2026-au) pricing and held off, Boxing Day is usually where the last meaningful discount of the year shows up before prices reset in the new year.

## What actually goes on sale

**Portable power stations** see the biggest genuine discounts — 20-30% off RRP is common on established brands clearing Christmas-period inventory. **Folding and rigid solar panels** discount more modestly, typically 10-15%, since margins are already thin. **CEC-listed grid-tied microinverter kits** rarely feature in Boxing Day promotions at all, since they're a smaller and more specialised category sold mostly through installers rather than big-box retail.

## Know what you're actually buying before the discount sways you

A Boxing Day price tag doesn't change what a product legally is in Australia. Before clicking through a banner:

- **Grid-tied solar still needs a CEC-approved inverter and a licensed electrician** under AS/NZS 4777 — a discounted plug-in kit does not change this requirement. See our [CEC-approved inverters guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-cec-approved-inverters-au) if a "plug and play" listing claims otherwise.

## After Boxing Day

If nothing on sale suits you, the next reliable discount window is typically late January around the Australia Day long weekend, though selection tends to be narrower than Boxing Day. Prices rarely move meaningfully in the first two weeks of January outside of that.

### Is Boxing Day actually cheaper than Black Friday for solar gear in Australia?

It varies by category. Power stations often see similar or slightly deeper discounts on Boxing Day since retailers are clearing Christmas inventory; panel-only kits tend to be discounted more consistently during Black Friday/Cyber Monday week. Worth comparing both before buying.

### Can I use a Boxing Day discount on a grid-tied inverter kit without an electrician?

No. Discount pricing has no bearing on the legal requirement for a CEC-approved inverter installed by a licensed electrician under AS/NZS 4777 for any grid-connected system. Portable, off-grid setups remain the self-install option.

### Do STC rebates apply to Boxing Day portable solar purchases?

Generally no — STC rebates apply to accredited, professionally installed systems, not portable power stations or plug-in kits bought at retail. Check eligibility before factoring a rebate into your Boxing Day budget.
