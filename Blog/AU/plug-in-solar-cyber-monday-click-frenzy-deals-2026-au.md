Australian shoppers get two major late-November sales events rather than one — Black Friday and Cyber Monday, imported from the US, plus Click Frenzy, which is a homegrown event that's been running locally for over a decade. For portable solar and power station buyers, that's three separate chances across about ten days to catch a genuine discount, and they don't all behave the same way. If you've already read our [Black Friday guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-black-friday-deals-2026-au), here's how Cyber Monday and Click Frenzy fit into the picture and whether either is worth waiting for.

## How Click Frenzy differs from the US-style sales

Click Frenzy is an Australian-run online shopping event, typically scheduled around the same mid-to-late November window as Black Friday, and it's structured more like a flash-sale marketplace than a single retailer's promotion. A number of solar and power station brands run dedicated Click Frenzy listings with limited-time pricing, separate from whatever they're doing for Black Friday or Cyber Monday on their own sites. The catch is that Click Frenzy deals can be genuinely time-limited — sometimes just a few hours — so if you're watching for a specific model, it pays to know roughly when the event's solar and outdoor-power category goes live rather than checking randomly.

Cyber Monday, by contrast, is the online-only follow-up to Black Friday and tends to run for the full day (or longer), with pricing from the likes of Amazon AU and direct brand stores. It's generally the more predictable of the two if you don't want to be glued to a countdown timer.

## What to check before buying from either event

Summer in Australia means panels are heading into their highest-output months, so getting a kit installed sooner rather than later has real value beyond just the discount. Before buying from a Click Frenzy or Cyber Monday listing, check:

- Whether the inverter is CEC-approved and meets [AS/NZS 4777](/au/blog/as-nzs-4777-plain-english-plug-in-solar-au) if you're planning any grid-tied use
- Total output against your actual summer load — our [sizing calculator](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-sizing-calculator-australia) is worth running before you buy based on price alone
- Whether a licensed electrician is required for your specific setup — see [DIY vs licensed electrician](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-diy-vs-licensed-electrician-australia)
- Delivery timing into regional areas — Click Frenzy's short-window deals sometimes ship from limited stock, which can mean longer delivery for buyers outside capital cities

## Which event actually gives the better price?

There's no consistent winner — it depends on the brand. Some sellers treat Click Frenzy as their single biggest discount of the year because it's a dedicated traffic event they pay to be featured in; others barely participate and save their real pricing for Cyber Monday instead. The practical approach is to check both rather than assume one beats the other, and to prioritise getting a CEC-approved, AS/NZS 4777-compliant kit over chasing the absolute lowest number. With summer generation about to peak, a kit bought and installed in late November will have paid back a meaningful chunk of its cost before autumn, especially if you're on a [Solar Sponge](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-nsw-solar-sponge-au) style tariff that rewards midday use.

If you miss both events, don't panic — Boxing Day sales in Australia are also significant, and the summer generation window stays strong well into February regardless of when you bought.

## A note for Grey Nomads and caravan buyers

If you're shopping for a [caravan or camping setup](/au/blog/camping-caravan-plug-in-solar-grey-nomad-au) rather than a fixed home installation, both events are worth watching slightly differently — portable, foldable panel kits and lightweight power stations tend to be the categories brands push hardest during Click Frenzy specifically, since it's an online-first event and those products ship easily without a tradie involved. A fixed, grid-connected garden or balcony kit is more likely to be the Cyber Monday or Black Friday hero product instead, since those listings lean on the bigger-ticket, AS/NZS 4777-compliant systems that need an electrician regardless of when you buy.

### Is Click Frenzy worth tracking separately from Cyber Monday?

Yes, if you're after a specific brand or model — some sellers reserve their steepest discounts exclusively for their Click Frenzy listing rather than running the same deal across both events.

### Do I need a licensed electrician for a Cyber Monday or Click Frenzy solar kit?

It depends on how you're using it. Fully portable, off-grid setups generally don't require one, but any grid-connected inverter must be CEC-approved and installed to AS/NZS 4777 by a licensed electrician.

### Will buying in late November give me a full summer of generation?

Mostly yes — with Australian summer generation running from roughly November through February, a kit installed in late November captures nearly the entire peak season.
