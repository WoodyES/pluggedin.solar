Black Friday has become one of the biggest buying windows of the year for portable solar panels and power stations in Australia, arriving right as the summer sun season properly kicks in. If you've been putting off a purchase while working out [whether plug-in solar is worth it](/au/blog/is-solar-worth-it-australia-2026) or [whether it's even legal to grid-tie](/au/blog/is-plug-in-solar-legal-australia), late November is typically when the best pricing of the year shows up — both from major retailers and directly from brands. Here's what to expect this year and how to avoid turning a good deal into a wasted purchase.

## Why Black Friday is genuinely different for solar kit pricing

Unlike a lot of Black Friday "deals" that are just regular pricing with a bigger red sticker, portable power stations and compact solar panel kits tend to see real discounts in this window — often 20-30% off list price. Brands like EcoFlow, Anker, Jackery and Bluetti use the pre-Christmas sales period to move current-generation stock ahead of new releases, which works in buyers' favour if you don't need the newest model on the shelf. If you're new to how these kits actually work in Australia, our [complete guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-australia-complete-guide) and [CEC-approved inverters guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-cec-approved-inverters-au) are worth a read before you buy anything, sale or not.

## What's actually worth buying in this category

Because grid-tied plug-in solar sits outside the [AS/NZS 4777](/au/blog/as-nzs-4777-plain-english-plug-in-solar-au) framework in Australia unless installed by a licensed electrician with a CEC-approved inverter, the practical Black Friday purchase for most households is a portable solar panel plus power station combo — used off-grid, for camping, blackout backup, or simply running smaller daytime loads without touching the switchboard at all. These setups don't need DNO-style grid approval the way a plug-in kit does in the UK, which makes them a genuinely simple purchase here. ## Jackery vs EcoFlow vs Bluetti: who tends to discount hardest

In past sale periods, Jackery and EcoFlow have both run aggressive bundle pricing — panel plus power station together, discounted further than either item alone — while Bluetti has tended to focus discounts on its larger capacity units. Our [Jackery vs EcoFlow comparison](/au/blog/jackery-vs-ecoflow-australia) and [Bluetti Elite 200 V2 review](/au/blog/bluetti-elite-200-v2-review-australia) both break down the actual spec differences so a sale price doesn't distract from buying the wrong capacity for your needs. If you're shopping specifically for a caravan or camping setup ahead of the summer holiday season, our [Grey Nomad guide](/au/blog/camping-caravan-plug-in-solar-grey-nomad-au) covers sizing for that use case specifically.

## Watch out for off-brand "solar kits" with no real spec sheet

Black Friday also brings out a wave of generic, unbranded solar kits on marketplaces with vague wattage claims and no real certification detail. If a listing doesn't clearly state panel wattage, battery chemistry (LiFePO4 is the standard for a reason), and inverter output, treat the "deal" with suspicion — a cheap kit that underperforms its claimed spec isn't a deal at all. Our [cheapest solar panels](/au/blog/cheapest-solar-panels-australia-2026) guide covers how to tell a genuine budget option from an underspecified one.

## Timing your purchase around summer performance

Buying in late November means a new kit will be generating through Australia's strongest sun months almost immediately, which is a meaningfully better outcome than buying the same kit in autumn and waiting months for decent output. If you're in a heat-affected region, it's worth reading our [Queensland heat and temperature coefficient guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-queensland-heat-temperature-coefficient-au) first, since panel efficiency actually drops somewhat in extreme heat — a factor worth knowing before you assume a bigger panel always means proportionally more output.

### Is Black Friday really the best time to buy portable solar in Australia?

For most buyers, yes — alongside the Boxing Day sales period, it's one of the two biggest discount windows of the year for this category, and buying now means getting full use of the kit through summer.

### Should I buy a bundle deal or separate panel and power station?

Bundles are usually cheaper if the included panel wattage suits your needs, but check the bundled panel's wattage against what you'd buy separately — some bundles pair a premium power station with an undersized panel to hit a lower headline price.

### Do portable power station deals apply to grid-tied solar too?

No — Black Friday deals in this category are almost entirely portable, off-grid gear. A genuine grid-tied system under AS/NZS 4777 still needs a licensed electrician and isn't something you buy off a retail sale page.
