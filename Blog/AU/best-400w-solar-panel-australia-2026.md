## What actually matters in AU conditions

**Temperature coefficient.** Every panel loses output when hot. On a 40°C Brisbane summer day cell temperatures can reach 65°C+ — reducing output by 12–16%. See our [Queensland heat guide](/au/blog/plug-in-solar-queensland-heat-temperature-coefficient-au) for the full physics. Look for a coefficient of -0.30%/°C or better (Trina, EcoFlow).

**AS/NZS 3760/5033 compliance for wiring.** If you're wiring into a home switchboard (not off-grid), you need a CEC-approved inverter and a licensed electrician — the panel itself is only part of the compliance story. See our [AS/NZS 4777 plain English guide](/au/blog/as-nzs-4777-plain-english-plug-in-solar-au).

**Frame quality for outback conditions.** Cheap panels use thin aluminium frames that flex and delaminate in extreme heat. Tier-1 frames (Renogy, Jinko, Trina) survive 10+ years in AU sun without warranty issues.

**MC4 vs Anderson plugs.** AU off-grid convention often uses Anderson plugs (SB50, SB120) rather than MC4. Most 400W panels ship with MC4 — you'll need adapter cables ($15–25) if integrating into an Anderson-based caravan wiring loom.

## Budget panels — a warning

Panels from unknown brands on Amazon and eBay sell for A$149–199. These are typically **rebranded factory-second panels** without tier-1 quality control. In AU conditions (heat, UV, dust) they degrade faster than tier-1 panels. Frame delamination is common in year 2–3.

A A$149 panel that generates 15% less than rated from year one is not saving you money. Stick with tier-1 brands (A$249+).

## Building a complete AU 800W setup

For an AU off-grid or portable 800W setup:

1. **1–2× 400W panels** (A$249–A$700 for tier-1)
2. **MPPT charge controller** (Victron 100/30 or similar, A$250–A$400) OR **portable power station** with built-in MPPT
3. **Mounting hardware** — tilt frame, roof rack or ground mount (A$100–A$300)
4. **MC4 to Anderson adapter** if integrating with existing caravan wiring (A$15–A$25)
5. **12V or 24V LiFePO4 battery** (A$800–A$2,500) OR **portable power station** — see [best portable power stations AU 2026](/au/blog/best-portable-power-stations-au-2026)

For grid-tied setups, remember AS/NZS 4777 requires CEC-approved inverter and licensed electrician — see our [regulatory guide](/au/blog/as-nzs-4777-plain-english-plug-in-solar-au). Off-grid setups have no such requirement.

### Can I install a 400W panel on my caravan or shed without an electrician?

For off-grid setups (panel to MPPT charge controller to house battery, or panel to portable power station), no electrician is required. Anderson plugs and DC wiring are unregulated. For any AC output (240V) or connection to the caravan's existing 240V wiring, a licensed electrician is required.
