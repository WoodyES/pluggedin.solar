## Getting Started with the EcoFlow App

The EcoFlow app is available for iOS and Android and provides real-time monitoring of your [STREAM plug-in solar kit](/blog/ecoflow-stream-uk-launch-september-2026). It shows live generation, historical data, battery status, and lets you configure smart scheduling.

## Initial Setup

1. **Download the EcoFlow app** from the App Store or Google Play
2. **Create an account** using your email address
3. **Power on your STREAM** by plugging it into a wall socket
4. **Open the app** and tap "Add Device"
5. **Select STREAM** from the device list
6. **Connect to WiFi** — the app will prompt you for your home network credentials
7. **Complete pairing** — the STREAM connects via Bluetooth first, then WiFi

Setup typically takes 2-3 minutes. If you have WiFi issues, check our [WiFi troubleshooting guide](/blog/plug-in-solar-wifi-connection-problems-uk).

## What the App Shows

### Real-Time Dashboard

- **Current generation** (watts) — updates every few seconds
- **Daily total** (kWh) — cumulative generation since midnight
- **Battery status** (if installed) — charge level, charging/discharging rate
- **Grid export estimate** — how much you're sending back to the grid

### Historical Data

- **Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly** generation charts
- **Peak generation time** — when your panels produce most
- **Self-consumption ratio** — percentage of generation used directly
- **Estimated savings** — calculated from your entered tariff rate

## Smart Scheduling (Battery Models)

If your STREAM includes the 600Wh battery, the app lets you set smart schedules:

- **Self-consumption mode**: Battery charges during peak solar, discharges in evening
- **Time-of-use mode**: Set charge/discharge windows to match your [energy tariff](/blog/best-energy-tariff-plug-in-solar-uk-2026)
- **Manual mode**: Set specific charge and discharge times

For [Octopus Agile](/blog/plug-in-solar-octopus-agile-uk) users, you can potentially align discharge with peak-rate periods to maximise savings.

## Tips for Accurate Monitoring

1. **Enter your tariff rate** in the app settings for accurate savings calculations
2. **Enable notifications** for generation milestones and system alerts
3. **Check weekly** to spot any unexpected drops in output
4. **Compare with [PVGIS estimates](/calculator)** to verify your system is performing as expected

### Why does my app show zero generation at night?

Solar panels only generate during daylight. Zero output at night is normal. If the app shows zero during a sunny day, check your [inverter connection](/blog/plug-in-solar-not-generating-troubleshooting-uk).

### Can I monitor from multiple devices?

Yes — log into your EcoFlow account on any device to see your STREAM data.

### Does the app work without WiFi?

The STREAM will generate power without WiFi, but monitoring data won't update in the app until connection is restored. Historical data is stored locally on the device and syncs when WiFi reconnects.
