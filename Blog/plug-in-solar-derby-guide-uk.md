## Why Derby Is a Decent Plug-In Solar Market

Derby sits at roughly 52.9°N, which puts it squarely in the "solidly average" band for UK solar — not as sunny as the south coast, not as grey as the far north of Scotland. For plug-in microgenerators under [SI 2026 No. 848](/blog/si-2026-848-plug-in-solar-explained-uk), that average is still good enough for a sensible payback, and Derby has a few local quirks worth knowing before you buy.

The city's housing stock — a mix of Victorian terraces around Normanton and Pear Tree, 1930s semis in Chaddesden and Allestree, and newer estates out towards Oakwood — gives most households either a south-facing garden fence, a rear elevation suitable for a wall-mounted panel, or a usable balcony on newer apartment blocks near the city centre. If you're not sure which of those applies to you, our [balcony vs garden vs roof comparison](/blog/balcony-vs-garden-vs-roof-plug-in-solar-uk) is worth five minutes before you order anything.

## Derby's Generation Numbers

An 800W plug-in system (two panels, one BS 1363 plug, within the 3.5A/800VA limit) in Derby typically produces:

| Season | Daily average | Monthly total |
|---|---|---|
| Summer (Jun-Aug) | 2.8-3.4 kWh | 85-105 kWh |
| Spring/Autumn | 1.6-2.2 kWh | 50-65 kWh |
| Winter (Dec-Feb) | 0.5-0.9 kWh | 15-27 kWh |

Annualised, that's roughly **780-830 kWh/year** — close to the Midlands average and noticeably better than Yorkshire or the North East. At Ofgem's current price cap of around 24.5p/kWh, that's approximately **£190-£205 a year** in avoided grid electricity, assuming decent self-consumption. Use the [800W payback calculator](/blog/plug-in-solar-payback-calculator-800w-uk) to plug in your own roof pitch and shading.

## Where to Mount It in Derby

- **South or south-west facing garden fence/wall** — the default choice for the Victorian terraces around Normanton; minimal shading from neighbouring roofs if your garden runs east-west.
- **Rear wall mount on semis in Chaddesden/Allestree** — most of these have unobstructed rear gardens with good solar access from mid-morning through to late afternoon.
- **Balcony rail mount** for the newer apartment developments along the Derwent near the city centre — see our [balcony rail mounting guide](/blog/balcony-rail-mounting-plug-in-solar) for the hardware you'll need.

Derby doesn't have the heavy tree cover that some Midlands suburbs do, but mature street trees in conservation-adjacent areas (parts of Duffield Road and the Cathedral Quarter) can clip afternoon generation — check your actual sun hours before buying rather than assuming from satellite imagery alone.

## DNO and Notification: Western Power Distribution

Derby sits in the East Midlands, served by **National Grid Electricity Distribution** (formerly Western Power Distribution's East Midlands licence area). Under the plug-in microgenerator rules, you still need to complete a simplified [G98 notification](/blog/g98-notification-guide-uk-homeowners) within 28 days of connection — it's a straightforward online form, not a site visit, and there's no fee for a single compliant 800VA unit.

## Buying Locally vs Online

Derby has a **B&Q** on Nottingham Road and a **Currys** at the Meteor Centre, both of which have started stocking entry-level plug-in kits since the law changed. For the full STREAM range or ENA-listed alternatives, online ordering from **Amazon UK**, **Screwfix** or direct from EcoFlow is usually faster and gives you access to the full range of panel counts. See our [retailer comparison](/blog/retailers-stocking-plug-in-solar-uk-2026) before you commit to one supplier.

Before buying any kit, check it against the live [ENA-compliant device registry](/blog/uk-compliant-plug-in-solar-devices-live) — not every product sold online carries a valid G98(GB) approval, and installing a non-compliant unit can invalidate your home insurance.

## Installation Timeline

Most Derby households get from unboxing to generating power in under two hours: panel mounting (30-45 minutes), running the cable to a suitable outdoor or garage socket, plugging in, and submitting the G98 notification online (10 minutes). Our [full installation guide](/blog/how-to-install-plug-in-solar-uk) walks through each step with photos.

### How much does plug-in solar cost to install in Derby?

An 800W kit typically costs £550-£850 depending on brand and retailer, with no professional installation fee required since it's a DIY plug-and-play system. Factor in the G98 notification (free) and you're looking at total costs matching the hardware price.

### Is Derby sunny enough for plug-in solar to be worth it?

Yes — Derby's generation is close to the UK average, and at current electricity prices an 800W system typically pays for itself in 4-6 years, then continues generating free electricity for the remainder of its 20+ year panel lifespan.

### Which DNO covers Derby for G98 notifications?

Derby is served by National Grid Electricity Distribution (East Midlands licence area). The notification process is identical regardless of which DNO covers your address — see our [step-by-step G98 guide](/blog/g98-dno-notification-step-by-step) for the exact form fields.
