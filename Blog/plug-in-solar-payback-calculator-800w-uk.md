## How Payback Works

Your payback period depends on three things: how much solar your location generates, how much of it you use directly, and what you pay per kWh. An 800W system typically pays for itself in **3-6 years** in the UK.

## Factors That Shorten Payback

1. **Higher self-consumption**: Working from home = 70-80% self-consumption = [faster payback](/blog/plug-in-solar-home-office-uk)
2. **South-facing placement**: [Optimal panel angle](/blog/plug-in-solar-panel-angle) at 30-35° maximises generation
3. **Higher energy tariff**: Every 1p/kWh increase shortens payback by ~6 months
4. **Lower kit cost**: [Early-bird pricing](/blog/ecoflow-stream-early-bird-pre-order-uk) or budget kits reduce initial investment

## Factors That Lengthen Payback

1. **Shading**: Even [partial shading](/blog/shading-plug-in-solar-output-uk) can halve output
2. **Non-optimal orientation**: [East-west facing](/blog/east-west-facing-plug-in-solar-uk) reduces annual output by 15-25%
3. **Low self-consumption**: Being out all day means more [export at lower rates](/blog/plug-in-solar-export-payments-seg-uk)
4. **Northern location**: Scotland generates ~15-20% less than the south

## 15-Year Return

Over 15 years (a conservative panel lifespan), an 800W system typically delivers:

| Scenario | Total savings | ROI on £600 kit |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Low (Scotland, out all day) | £900 | 50% |
| Average (Midlands, mixed presence) | £1,500 | 150% |
| High (South, mostly home) | £2,400 | 300% |

### Does panel degradation affect payback?

Panels degrade at roughly [0.5% per year](/blog/solar-panel-degradation-rate-uk). Over 15 years, that's about 7.5% total — included in our calculations but not a significant factor.

### What about maintenance costs?

[Maintenance](/blog/how-to-maintain-plug-in-solar-panels-uk) is minimal — occasional cleaning and visual inspection. Budget £10-20/year for [cleaning supplies](/blog/best-solar-panel-cleaning-tools-uk).

### Can I calculate payback for a 400W system?

Yes — halve the generation figures. Payback is similar in years because both the kit cost and savings are proportionally lower.
