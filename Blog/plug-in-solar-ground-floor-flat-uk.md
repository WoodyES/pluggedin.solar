## Ground Floor Advantages

Ground floor flats often have something upper-floor flats lack: **outdoor ground space**. A small patio, garden, or even a shared yard can support plug-in solar panels.

## Setup Options

### Patio Installation
Place panels on a south-facing patio using freestanding ground mounts. The [EcoFlow STREAM](/blog/ecoflow-stream-uk-launch-september-2026) includes ground stands that work on paving, decking, or [grass](/blog/solar-panels-on-decking).

### Small Garden
Even a 2m × 2m space can accommodate two 400W panels. Use [adjustable tilt brackets](/blog/best-adjustable-solar-panel-tilt-brackets-uk) to achieve the optimal 30-35° angle.

### Communal Garden
If your flat has a communal garden, you may need permission from other residents or the management company. Plug-in solar is portable and non-permanent, which helps your case.

### Wall-Mounted
A south-facing external wall can support [wall-mounted panels](/blog/wall-mount-plug-in-solar). Output is reduced (~70% of optimal) due to the near-vertical angle, but it uses no ground space.

## Cable Routing

The biggest practical challenge is getting the cable from outdoor panels to an indoor socket:

- **Through a window gap**: Flat ribbon cables designed for solar can pass through closed windows
- **Through a ventilation brick**: Route through an existing air vent
- **Through a drilled hole**: A 20mm hole sealed with an [IP68 cable gland](/blog/ip68-cable-glands-solar-uk) gives a clean, weatherproof entry

Remember: [no extension leads](/blog/plug-in-solar-no-extension-lead-rule-uk) — the panel must plug directly into a wall socket.

## Renter Considerations

As a ground floor [renter](/blog/plug-in-solar-for-renters-uk):

- Check your tenancy agreement for clauses about external fixtures
- Freestanding panels on a patio don't modify the building
- The [Renters' Rights Act 2025](/blog/renters-rights-act-2025-solar) supports reasonable requests
- You take the kit when you move — nothing is permanent

### What if my patio is north-facing?

See our [north-facing garden guide](/blog/plug-in-solar-north-facing-garden-uk). Flat-mounted panels still generate meaningful output.

### Can I store panels inside overnight?

You could, but it's impractical daily. Modern panels are designed for permanent outdoor installation and are waterproof (IP67/IP68). Just leave them outside.

### What if my ground floor flat has no outdoor space?

Without outdoor space, options are limited. Check if you can mount panels on your windowsill (very small output) or negotiate access to a communal area.
