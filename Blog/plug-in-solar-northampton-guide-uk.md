Northampton doesn't get talked about much in the plug-in solar conversation, but it's actually a strong candidate town for it. A mix of Victorian terraces near the town centre, 1930s semis in Kingsthorpe and Abington, and sprawling new-build estates out at Upton, Hunsbury and the Northampton Gateway developments means there's a huge range of south-facing gardens, balconies and outbuildings to work with — and [plug-in solar has been legal across England, Scotland and Wales](/blog/is-plug-in-solar-legal-uk) since 27 August 2026 under SI 2026 No. 848.

## How much will an 800W kit generate in Northampton?

Northampton sits at roughly 52.2°N, putting it firmly in the same irradiance band as the rest of the [East Midlands](/blog/plug-in-solar-midlands-guide-uk) — slightly ahead of Birmingham and Nottingham on PVGIS estimates because of marginally lower average rainfall. A well-sited, south-facing 800W kit should produce somewhere in the region of 840-870 kWh across the year, with the bulk of that landing between April and September. At a self-consumption rate of 50-60% and the current Ofgem price cap of roughly 24.5p/kWh, that works out to annual savings in the £105-£120 range — a payback period of around five years on a typical £600 kit.

## New builds vs older housing stock

West Northants' newer estates (Upton, Hunsbury Fields, the Northampton Gateway sites) tend to have smaller, more sheltered gardens and fewer overshading issues from mature trees, which actually makes them easier sites for a ground-mounted or wall-mounted kit than some of the older, tree-lined streets in Abington or Kingsthorpe. If you're in a newer estate house, a straightforward [ground-mounted kit](/blog/ground-mount-plug-in-solar) angled to the rear garden is usually the simplest route. In the Victorian terraces closer to the centre, roof pitch and chimney shading matter more — check our guide on [mounting on a pitched roof](/blog/mount-plug-in-solar-pitched-roof-uk) before committing to a spot.

## Renting in Northampton

Northampton has a large rental market, particularly around the university and the terraced streets off Wellingborough Road and St James. If you're renting, a [balcony or portable kit](/blog/balcony-solar-uk-guide) that doesn't require drilling into brickwork is the safest option, and it's worth reading our [renters' guide](/blog/plug-in-solar-for-renters-uk) first — landlord permission and tenancy-agreement wording still matter even though the device itself is now legal nationally.

## Registering with your DNO

Northampton falls under National Grid Electricity Distribution's East Midlands network. Even though plug-in microgenerators under the 800VA/3.5A limit don't need the full G99 application process that larger systems require, it's still worth working through our [G98 notification guide](/blog/g98-notification-guide-uk-homeowners) so your paperwork is in order from day one — it takes about ten minutes and avoids any confusion if you ever come to sell the house.

## Checking what you're buying

With plug-in solar now mainstream, the number of kits on the market has grown quickly, and not all of them meet the ENA's compliance requirements. Before you buy anything outside the well-known brands, cross-check the model against the [live ENA-compliant device registry](/blog/uk-compliant-plug-in-solar-devices-live) — it's updated regularly and is the fastest way to confirm a kit is genuinely G98-compliant rather than just claiming to be.

### Is Northampton a good location for plug-in solar?

Yes. It sits in the East Midlands irradiance band, which is above the UK average, and the mix of modern estates with unshaded gardens and older terraces with pitched roofs means most housing types in the town have at least one workable mounting option.

### Do I need planning permission in Northampton for a plug-in solar kit?

Generally no, provided the kit stays within the 800VA/3.5A plug-in microgenerator limit and you're not in a listed building or conservation area. Check with West Northamptonshire Council if your property has any specific restrictions.

### Where's the best place to buy a kit in Northampton itself?

B&Q, Currys and Screwfix all have branches inside the ring road, with Wickes a short drive out at Rushmills — all four are worth comparing in person before you commit to a specific model.
