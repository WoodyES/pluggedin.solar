Hull doesn't get mentioned in solar conversations as often as Leeds or Sheffield, but the city's flat terrain, wide estuary skyline and lack of overshadowing hills actually make it a decent candidate for [plug-in solar](/blog/is-plug-in-solar-legal-uk). Since the technology became legal nationwide on 27 August 2026 under SI 2026 No. 848, interest has been building steadily in East Yorkshire — and the numbers back it up.

## Hull's Solar Numbers

Hull sits at roughly 53.75°N, putting it firmly in the same generation band as the rest of [Yorkshire](/blog/plug-in-solar-yorkshire-guide-uk). An 800W plug-in kit here typically produces **810-860 kWh per year**, slightly ahead of inland Yorkshire cities thanks to lower cloud cover off the Humber and fewer tall buildings casting shade on south-facing gardens.

## Hull's Housing Stock Suits Plug-In Solar Well

A large share of Hull's housing is pre-war [terraced](/blog/plug-in-solar-terraced-houses-uk) and [ex-council stock](/blog/plug-in-solar-ex-council-homes-uk), much of it with rear yards or small gardens rather than large roofs suited to traditional rooftop solar. That's precisely the housing type plug-in solar was designed around — no scaffolding, no roof penetration, and no requirement to own freehold roof space. A single 400W panel against a south or west-facing rear wall, or two panels on a simple A-frame ground mount, is realistic in the vast majority of Hull's terraced streets.

Newer estates around Kingswood and the outskirts tend to have more garden space, making an 800W dual-panel setup — or the larger [EcoFlow STREAM Ultra](https://www.awin1.com/cread.php?awinmid=51797&awinaffid=2846734&ued=https%3A%2F%2Fuk.ecoflow.com%2Fproducts%2Fstream-ultra-pro) — more practical.

## Coastal Air and the Humber Estuary

Hull's position on the Humber means salt-laden air is a genuine factor, more than it would be for, say, Leeds or York. This doesn't rule out plug-in solar, but it does affect which hardware and mounting kit you choose. Read our guide on [coastal installations and salt corrosion](/blog/plug-in-solar-coastal-areas-salt-corrosion-uk) before buying — stainless steel fixings and IP66+ rated junction boxes are worth the small extra cost in this kind of environment, especially for kit installed within a mile or two of the estuary.

## Getting the Paperwork Right

Plug-in solar in Hull — as anywhere in Great Britain — needs a G98 notification to your Distribution Network Operator before you switch on. Hull falls under Northern Powergrid's distribution area. The process is quick, free, and online; our [step-by-step G98 notification guide](/blog/g98-notification-guide-uk-homeowners) walks through exactly what Hull households need to submit.

Make sure whatever kit you buy appears on the [live ENA-compliant device registry](/blog/uk-compliant-plug-in-solar-devices-live) before you notify your DNO. Non-compliant kits — often cheaper imports sold without proper certification — can delay or invalidate your notification, and some insurers won't cover them.

## Preparing for Hull's Winter

Hull's winters bring strong easterly winds off the North Sea and the Humber, which is actually good news for panel cleanliness — wind-driven rain keeps surfaces clearer than in sheltered inland gardens. That said, make sure ground-mounted frames are properly weighted or staked; see our [winter preparation guide](/blog/plug-in-solar-winter-preparation-2026-uk) for a pre-season checklist that's particularly relevant to exposed estuary-facing gardens.

## Is It Worth It in Hull?

For most terraced and semi-detached households in Hull, yes — the combination of flat terrain, minimal overshadowing, and lower property/garden-space constraints than many southern cities makes the economics work out favourably. Payback on an 800W kit sits comfortably under four years at current Ofgem price cap rates, and that only improves if prices rise further.

### Does Hull get enough sun for plug-in solar to be worthwhile?

Yes. Hull's latitude and flat, low-shading terrain put it on par with the rest of Yorkshire for solar generation — around 810-860 kWh/year from an 800W kit, enough for a sub-four-year payback at current electricity prices.

### Do I need special equipment because Hull is coastal?

Not special equipment exactly, but it's worth prioritising stainless steel fixings and higher IP-rated enclosures given the salt air off the Humber, particularly for installations within a mile or two of the estuary. See our [coastal corrosion guide](/blog/plug-in-solar-coastal-areas-salt-corrosion-uk) for specifics.

### Which DNO covers Hull for G98 notifications?

Hull falls under Northern Powergrid. The notification process is free and typically completed online within a few minutes — see our [G98 guide](/blog/g98-notification-guide-uk-homeowners) for the full steps.
