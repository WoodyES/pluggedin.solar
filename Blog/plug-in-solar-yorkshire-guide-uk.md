## Yorkshire Solar Numbers

Yorkshire (53-54°N) generates roughly **780-840 kWh/year** from an 800W system. That's 5-10% less than the Midlands but still solidly positive economics.

| City | Annual kWh | Annual saving | Payback (£600) |
|------|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Leeds | 815 | £110 | 5.5 years |
| Sheffield | 820 | £111 | 5.4 years |
| York | 825 | £111 | 5.4 years |
| Bradford | 800 | £108 | 5.6 years |
| Hull | 830 | £112 | 5.4 years |

Use our [calculator](/calculator) for your exact postcode.

## Yorkshire Advantages

- **Lower property prices** = lower opportunity cost for garden space
- **Strong winds** = panels stay clean (but need [storm-proofing](/blog/how-to-storm-proof-your-plug-in-solar-panels))
- **Long summer days** at higher latitude = more generation hours in June/July

### Is Sheffield too hilly for solar?

Hills don't affect solar output directly — it's about panel orientation and [shading](/blog/shading-plug-in-solar-output-uk). Sheffield's hills may actually help by providing south-facing slopes.

### How does Yorkshire compare to Germany?

Yorkshire at 54°N is similar to Hamburg (53.5°N), where plug-in solar is extremely popular. The economics work.
