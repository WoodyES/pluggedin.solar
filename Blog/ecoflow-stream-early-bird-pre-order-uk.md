## Early-Bird Pre-Order Details

EcoFlow has opened an early-bird pre-order window for the [STREAM 800W plug-in solar kit](/blog/ecoflow-stream-uk-launch-september-2026), running from **12 August to 15 September 2026**.

Pre-order customers get priority shipping and discounted pricing — typically **10-20% below** the expected retail price.

## How to Pre-Order

1. Visit the [EcoFlow UK online store](https://www.awin1.com/cread.php?awinmid=51797&awinaffid=2846734&ued=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ecoflow.com%2Fuk%2Fstream-balcony-solar-system)
2. Select your STREAM configuration (panels only, with battery, with mount)
3. Complete checkout — you won't be charged until the kit ships
4. Kits ship from September 2026

## Is It Worth Pre-Ordering?

**Pros:**
- Best launch pricing — unlikely to be this cheap again until Black Friday
- Priority shipping — among the first in the UK to install legal plug-in solar
- The [EcoFlow End of Summer Sale](/blog/ecoflow-end-of-summer-sale-2026-uk) may offer additional savings on accessories

**Cons:**
- No independent UK reviews yet
- Other manufacturers may launch competing kits at lower prices
- Certification isn't confirmed — though EcoFlow has been working with DESNZ

## Combine with the Summer Sale

EcoFlow's [End of Summer Sale](/blog/ecoflow-end-of-summer-sale-2026-uk) (10–31 August) offers up to 50% off panels and accessories. While the STREAM itself may not be in the sale, you can pick up complementary items at a discount.

### What's the cancellation policy?

EcoFlow typically allows free cancellation of pre-orders before shipping. Check the specific terms at the time of ordering.

### Will pre-order kits be IPS v2 certified?

EcoFlow has been working with DESNZ on certification. Pre-order kits are expected to ship with full certification, but confirm this before the ship date.

### Can I pre-order from Amazon instead?

Amazon may also offer pre-orders, but early-bird pricing is typically only available through EcoFlow's direct store.
