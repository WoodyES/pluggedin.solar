## Midlands Solar Potential

The Midlands sits at 52-53°N latitude, generating slightly less solar energy than the South but more than Northern England or Scotland. An 800W system here produces roughly **820-880 kWh/year** — solid returns.

## Midlands-Specific Tips

### Weather Patterns
The Midlands has relatively average cloud cover — less coastal fog than the east coast, less rain than the west. Spring and summer months are particularly productive.

### Housing Stock
The Midlands has a high proportion of [semi-detached](/blog/plug-in-solar-semi-detached-uk) and [terraced houses](/blog/plug-in-solar-terraced-houses-uk) with rear gardens — ideal for [ground-mounted](/blog/ground-mount-plug-in-solar) plug-in solar.

### East Midlands vs West Midlands
Marginal difference (~2-3%) in favour of the East Midlands due to slightly less rainfall. Both regions are excellent for plug-in solar.

### How does the Midlands compare to London?

Very similar — London generates ~5-8% more due to being slightly further south, but the difference is £5-10/year in savings.

### Is plug-in solar worth it in the Midlands?

Absolutely. With payback periods of 5-5.5 years and 15-year net savings of £1,100+, the Midlands is excellent territory for plug-in solar.
