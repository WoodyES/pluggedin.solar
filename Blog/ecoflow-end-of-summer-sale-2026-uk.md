1. **Sign up for EcoFlow's newsletter** — they often send exclusive codes to subscribers

## Is It Worth Buying Now?

If you're planning a plug-in solar setup for when [kits launch in September](/blog/plug-in-solar-legal-27-august-2026-uk), buying panels and accessories now at sale prices can reduce your total cost. However, buying panels separately won't create a [certified plug-in microgenerator](/blog/plug-in-microgenerator-uk-what-is-it) — you'll still need a certified complete kit for legal compliance.

### Will the STREAM be in the sale?

Unlikely, as it launches after the sale ends. The [early-bird pre-order](/blog/ecoflow-stream-early-bird-pre-order-uk) is the closest equivalent.

### How does this compare to Amazon Prime Day deals?

EcoFlow's own sales typically match or beat Prime Day pricing, with the advantage of longer availability and direct warranty support.
