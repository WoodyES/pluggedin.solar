## The Regulatory Question

[SI 2026 No. 848](/blog/si-2026-848-plug-in-solar-explained-uk) applies to **England, Wales, and Scotland**. Northern Ireland has separate product safety regulations administered by the Department for the Economy.

As of August 2026, Northern Ireland has not yet made equivalent legislation. This means plug-in solar kits cannot legally be sold or marketed for plug-in use in NI under current regulations.

## What's Expected

The NI Executive is expected to consult on equivalent legislation in late 2026 or early 2027. The technical requirements will likely mirror the [Interim Product Specification](/blog/interim-product-specification-v2-plug-in-solar-uk) to ensure UK-wide product compatibility.

## Generation Potential

Northern Ireland at 54-55°N generates roughly **760-820 kWh/year** from an 800W system — similar to [Yorkshire](/blog/plug-in-solar-yorkshire-guide-uk).

### Can I buy a kit from England and use it in NI?

The kit itself isn't illegal to own, but it cannot legally be plugged into a socket under current NI regulations. Wait for NI-specific legislation.

### When will NI legalise plug-in solar?

No confirmed date, but late 2026 or early 2027 is expected based on current political signals.
