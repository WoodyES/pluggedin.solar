## The Budget Plug-In Solar Market

As [plug-in solar becomes legal on 27 August 2026](/blog/plug-in-solar-legal-27-august-2026-uk), budget kits under £500 will be the entry point for most UK households. Here's what's available and expected.

## What to Look For Under £500

### Must-haves
- **IPS v2 certification** — legal compliance from 27 August
- **Anti-islanding protection** — safety requirement
- **BS 1363 plug with 5A fuse** — UK regulatory requirement
- **At least 400W output** — anything less isn't worth the investment

### Nice-to-haves
- App-based monitoring (saves buying a separate [smart plug](/blog/best-smart-plug-monitor-solar-uk))
- Adjustable mounting brackets
- Weatherproof connectors
- 5+ year warranty

## Our Recommendation

For under £500, the [EcoFlow STREAM 800W at early-bird pricing](/blog/ecoflow-stream-early-bird-pre-order-uk) offers the best combination of output, legality, and features. If you need to go even cheaper, wait for Lidl's budget kit — expected autumn 2026.

### Is a 400W kit worth it?

A 400W kit saves roughly half what an 800W kit saves (£75-125/year vs £150-250/year). The payback period is similar, but the absolute savings are lower. If budget is tight, 400W is still worthwhile.

### Should I buy cheap panels now and upgrade later?

You can, but mixing panels with a certified kit may void the certification. It's better to buy a complete certified system from the start.

### Will prices drop further?

Competition will drive prices down through 2027. But waiting means missing months of savings — our [ROI calculator](/blog/plug-in-solar-roi-calculator-uk) can help you decide.
